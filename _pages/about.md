I am 38 years old and live in Guimarães, Portugal.
I am a focused person who likes to achieve my goals and overcome all obstacles to reach them. In addition, I am a very responsible and professional person. I enjoy working in a team and when it is necessary to lead.

I completed my Master's Degree in Information Systems Engineering and Management at the end of 2022 with the presentation of my dissertation: Evaluation of the Success of Big Data Information Technology Applications. 
My education began in high school with a focus on Electronics, but then in higher education I pursued this Master's degree based on Information Systems, where I focused more on specialising in Big Data, BI, and Software Architecture. I intend to redirect my career towards Data Analysis and/or Business Intelligence, applying knowledge in SQL Server, ETL, MS Visual Studio, and Tableau to generate business insights and support decision-making.

Professionally, I have worked for several companies in various industries, which has allowed me to evolve and mature over time. My main positions at the start of my career in Information Systems were as a website content manager ([decathlon.pt](https://www.decathlon.pt), [myprivateboutique.ch](https://www.myprivateboutique.ch)), where I have over 4 years of experience.

In academic terms, I have carried out several projects in various areas of IT, which are described in more detail in the projects section. This includes my dissertation, which focused on building a model for evaluating information systems. It was a pioneering study in Portugal on this type of evaluation, which can subsequently be adapted to other areas such as auditing, management, etc.
